About the Workshop

The Southeast Applied and Computational Math (ACM) Student Workshop is a premier venue for graduate students, postdocs, and researchers to exchange ideas, showcase local research, and foster collaborations across the Southeast US.

Featured Session: Careers and Research Opportunities in Scientific Computing and Data Science.
