Hotels and Travel

Emory University is located in the historic Druid Hills neighborhood of Atlanta.

Nearby Hotels

  • University Inn at Emory
    Closest option, walking distance to campus.
  • Emory Conference Center Hotel
    Premium on-campus accommodation.
  • Courtyard Atlanta Decatur
    Downtown Decatur, short shuttle ride.
Additional options available in Decatur and Midtown areas.

Transportation

From ATL Airport

Rideshare services (Uber/Lyft/Taxi) are the most convenient and direct way to reach the Emory area from Hartsfield-Jackson Airport (approx. 20-30 mins).

Public Transit

MARTA light rail and bus services connect Emory to the rest of Atlanta. Visit itsmarta.com for schedules.
