Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp

A recurring Atlanta-area program for undergraduate and high school students who want to explore the mathematics behind modern data science and machine learning.

2026 Bootcamp 2025 Emory Archive All Workshops
Audience Undergraduate and high school students
Format Tutorials and group projects
Locations Emory University and Georgia Tech

About the Series

The bootcamp introduces students to mathematical ideas and computational tools that shape data science and machine learning. The series began at Emory University in 2025 and continued at Georgia Tech in 2026.

Offerings

Second Offering at Georgia Tech

Georgia Tech, Atlanta. September 19-20, 2026.

The 2026 bootcamp is hosted at Georgia Tech and includes tutorials, a group project day, and free participation with lunch and parking provided.

First Offering at Emory

Emory University, Atlanta. April 12-13, 2025.

The inaugural bootcamp featured tutorials on PyTorch programming, computer vision, generative models, and scientific machine learning, followed by a group project competition.
