Transportation from ATL Airport

    Uber/Lyft/Taxi fares are approximately $40 from the airport to midtown Atlanta. If you are arriving at the domestic terminal, the MARTA light rail has a station at the terminal that goes directly to North Avenue and Midtown Stations in 20 minutes (plus typical 10 minute wait time) and costs $2.50. If you are arriving internationally at the international terminal (Concourse F), it is usually more convenient to take Uber/Lyft, but it is also possible to take the airport's free shuttle bus from the international terminal to the domestic terminal (15 minutes plus wait time) and the airport MARTA station. For more information: visit itsmarta.com
