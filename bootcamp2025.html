Atlanta · April 12-13, 2025

Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp

The first Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp is scheduled for April 12-13 at Emory University for undergraduate and high school students interested in machine learning, data science, and scientific machine learning.

Schedule Flyer
Dates Saturday-Sunday, April 12-13, 2025
Time 9:00 AM-5:00 PM
Location E208, MSC, Emory University
Format Tutorials and group project competition

About

The bootcamp will feature a one-day tutorial covering PyTorch programming, computer vision, generative models, and scientific machine learning, followed by a day dedicated to a group project competition. Lunch will be provided on both days.

Data Science Machine Learning Students Emory

Program Format

Day one focuses on tutorials introducing tools and ideas from modern machine learning. Day two is devoted to a group project competition and student presentations.
