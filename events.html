Events and Outreach

Selected local, regional, and international workshops, conferences, student programs, and public outreach activities that I have co-organized, including several continuing event series.

Upcoming

Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp

Second offering. Georgia Tech, Atlanta. September 19-20, 2026.

Recurring Atlanta-area program for undergraduate and high school students interested in the mathematics behind data science and machine learning. The inaugural bootcamp was held at Emory in 2025; the 2026 offering is hosted at Georgia Tech and includes tutorials, a group project day, and free participation with lunch and parking provided.

Local SeriesData ScienceStudents

Numerical Linear Algebra in the Era of AI

ICERM, Providence, Rhode Island. May 17-21, 2027.

Topical workshop bringing together researchers from mathematics, computer science, engineering, and industry to discuss numerical linear algebra in the AI era. Themes include iterative methods and preconditioning for large-scale optimization, matrix and tensor methods, randomized and data-driven algorithms, learning-augmented numerical methods, and hardware-aware computation.

Community Outreach

Atlanta Science Festival

Piedmont Park, Atlanta. 2024; planned return in 2027, 2028, and 2029.

Our group participated in the Atlanta Science Festival at Piedmont Park with the Emory Mathematics booth “Math Makes It Happen!”, sharing hands-on mathematics activities with families, students, and the broader Atlanta community. We plan to return in 2027, 2028, and 2029.

Recent and Past

Southeast Applied and Computational Math Student Workshop

Fourth annual workshop. Emory University, Atlanta. April 3-4, 2026.

Annual Southeast workshop for graduate students, postdocs, and researchers in applied and computational mathematics. The 2026 Emory workshop included plenary talks, student research presentations, and a featured session on careers and research opportunities in scientific computing and data science.

Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp at Emory

First offering. Emory University, Atlanta. April 12-13, 2025.

Inaugural bootcamp in the Atlanta Mathematics of Data Science Bootcamp series, featuring tutorials on PyTorch programming, computer vision, generative models, and scientific machine learning, followed by a group project competition.

Precond24

13th International Conference on Preconditioning Techniques for Scientific and Industrial Applications. Georgia Tech, Atlanta. June 10-12, 2024.

International conference series focused on preconditioning techniques for sparse and structured matrix computations. The 2024 meeting included themes such as machine learning and preconditioning, inverse problems, randomized algorithms, mixed precision, domain decomposition, multigrid, and high-performance software.
